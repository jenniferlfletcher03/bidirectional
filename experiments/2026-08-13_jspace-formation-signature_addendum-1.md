## Part A — Rung A's trained corpus (Gap A)

**A1 — Ruling.** Rung A's trained condition trains on **Arm 1's corpus** (naive demonstrations — behavior displayed, no reasons).

**A2 — Rationale.** The steering vector is derived from contrastive pairs, which are demonstration-grade material by nature. Training the comparison condition on the same content grade gives both installation routes the same starter material; the residual difference between Rung A's conditions is route and only route. This is the Arm 2 derivation rule's logic (sealed §5), transposed up a rung, and it preserves §3's sealed framing of Rung A as a pure route-of-acquisition question.

**A3 — Roads not taken.** Arm 2's why-enriched corpus was rejected: it confounds installation route with content grade — any Rung A difference would have two suspects and no way to interrogate them separately. A dedicated new corpus was rejected: a third authoring project with a fresh confound surface and no symmetry gain.

**A4 — Ingredients rule.** The steering vector's contrastive pairs are drawn from Arm 1's own scenario pool, with the holdout lint applied (no pair touches authority, consensus, or stakes/incentive material). The raw ingredients, not merely the content grade, are identical across routes.

**A5 — The twins.** All twelve sealed LoRA runs (§8.1) execute. The three Rung A trained runs (Arm 1 corpus, seeds 13/42/2026) are computationally identical to Arm 1's three Rung B runs and are **registered as a pipeline determinism check**: any divergence between "identical" twins measures the pipeline's nondeterminism floor, which is reported before seed variance is interpreted anywhere in the analysis. The sealed count of twelve reads literally.

**A6 — Conflict-of-interest note.** The consultant's registered Rung A lean (§7.4, item 4) is that the steered condition is brittle. The rejected Arm 2 corpus, as the strongest trained comparator, would have made that lean easier to confirm; the consultant argued for the corpus that handicaps his own registered bet. Noted so the record shows the direction the conflict cut.

## Part B — The steered condition's gate functions (Gap B)

**B1 — Strength and layer frozen blind.** Steering strength and injection layer are chosen on a **calibration slice**: scenarios disjoint from the enumerated battery, drawn under the §10 one-pass discipline (calibration stimuli are logged as spent). Tuning targets a single criterion — matching the flashcards-trained twin's on-distribution covered-type hold-rate, per §8.3's equivalence math — and both parameters are then frozen at corpus freeze alongside everything else. No post-hoc strength adjustment exists in this design.

**B2 — Capability floor, transposed.** Base-plus-vector, at the frozen strength, must satisfy the same capability floor the trained conditions face (§8.4.1: within 5 IFEval points of the base instruct checkpoint). Otherwise the steered condition is void. Without this gate, "steering is brittle" could mean only "we steered hard enough to break the model."

**B3 — Three vectors.** The steered condition trains nothing, so seed variance transposes to the vector's ingredients: three vectors are derived from three independent resamples of the contrastive-pair pool, using the sealed seed values 13, 42, 2026 (same labels as §8.1; the referent transposes from initialization to pair draw). The steered condition's min-to-max range enters the §8.2 non-overlap gate under the same rule as every trained condition. One ledger, no special columns.

**B4 — Vector on throughout.** The steering vector is applied continuously through the entire battery conversation — pressure junctures **and recovery junctures**. Sealed explicitly because the consultant's registered recovery prediction (§7.4, item 4: "the value gone the moment the vector stops holding it") would otherwise be confirmable by construction. If the steered value fails recovery while the vector is still pulling, that is a real result; nothing weaker counts.

**B5 — Named exit.** If no strength exists that both matches the twin (B1) and clears the floor (B2), that is a registered finding — *contrastive steering cannot cleanly install the target behavior at acceptable capability cost at this scale* — reported in the §9 named-exit style, not narrated after the fact.

**B5 contingency (ruled 2026-08-13).** If B5 fires, **Rung B proceeds**, contingent on the §8.4.2 manipulation check as always. Rationale: a void on Rung A is a finding about steering, not about the pipeline; it gates Rung B no harder than the null the sealed §4 tree already governs.
